Framework & Classification

🏷️ Understanding Establishment vs. Progressive Alignment

Candidates are categorized into clear ideological factions based on funding sources, endorsement networks, policy platforms, and alignment with local political power structures:

Progressive

Backed by labor unions (SEIU, PCTA), grassroots donors, and progressive PACs. Prioritizes civil rights, explicit LGBTQ+ advocacy, health equity, housing justice, and anti-censorship.

Establishment

Backed by party leaders, corporate PACs, real estate networks (STAR), and utility interests. Focuses on pragmatic administration, major economic development, and party caucus stability.

Conservative Establishment / Right-Wing

Backed by official GOP machinery and culture-war PACs (Moms for Liberty, 1776 Project). Emphasizes parental rights, lower taxes, and restricting LGBTQ+ school curricula.

Nonpartisan / Moderate

Judicial candidates bound by judicial ethics or hyper-local civic figures focused strictly on municipal services, stormwater infrastructure, and public safety.

QAnon & Conspiracies

Investigative reports uncovered social media videos of Peters reciting the QAnon oath. Peters also amplified conspiracy theories calling the 9/11 attacks, moon landing, and COVID-19 pandemic "hoaxes," and referencing election denial as "Trump 2Q2Q."

Charlie Crist (St. Pete Mayor)

Utility Money & Empty Chair

Crist accepted >$130,000 from utility dark money tied to FPL and Duke Energy while St. Petersburg conducts a study to sever ties with Duke. Crist was also criticized for skipping the Suncoast Tiger Bay Club debate, leaving an empty chair on stage.

Source: Local Campaign Finance Audits & Tiger Bay Debate

Mark Bunker (Clearwater Seat 5)

Scientology Base Split

80% of Bunker's funds ($29k) come from out-of-state anti-Scientology donors. His donor coalition suffered chaos after key ally Aaron Smith-Levin was removed from the anti-Scientology Aftermath Foundation following misconduct allegations.

Source: Clearwater Municipal Reports & Finance Analysis

Ken Welch (St. Pete Mayor)

Major Project Collapses

Welch's administration suffered the late-stage collapse of the multi-billion dollar Tampa Bay Rays stadium / Gas Plant District redevelopment deal, alongside the breakdown of a proposed Moffitt Cancer Center campus downtown over subsidy disagreements.
